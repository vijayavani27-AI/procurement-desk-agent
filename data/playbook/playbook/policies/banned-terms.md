# Banned contract terms (escalate to Legal)

- **Automatic renewal** in any wording (auto-renews, continues year to year unless
  cancelled, renews on its own).
- **Upfront or advance payment above 30%** of order value (in numbers or words).
- **Unlimited liability for Acme.**
- **Exclusivity:** Acme must buy only from this supplier.

Allowed: renewal only by mutual agreement, advance or upfront payments of 30% or less,
net-30/45/60 terms, warranties, delivery dates, and statements that no exclusivity is asked.
